# Sales Outreach Contact Plan

Updated: 2026-06-11

## Objective

Send a targeted, professional preview of the independent handball matchcenter
prototype and request a 20-minute feedback discussion. The first outreach goal
is product feedback and routing to the correct decision maker, not a public
launch or a hard sales close.

## Recommended First Recipients

### 1. OEHB Office

Use OEHB as the first path because OEHB is the public content and association
owner for Austrian handball context.

- Public contact source: `https://www.oehb.at/de/kontakt`
- Public imprint source: `https://www.oehb.at/de/impressum`
- Public office source: `https://www.oehb.at/de/oehb/office`
- Known public office email from source review: `oehb@oehb.at`

Recommended message:

- To: OEHB office contact
- Optional CC: OEHB competition coordination, only if still listed on the
  current OEHB office page at send time.
- Ask: "Could you forward this to the person responsible for digital matchcenter
  or nuLiga product coordination?"

## Message Boundaries

Say:

- Independent prototype.
- Mobile-first UX proposal.
- Fixture-backed reviewed preview.
- No official status.
- No productive integration yet.
- No contacts, phone numbers, emails, referee data, login-protected data, or
  unrelated personal fields.
- U18 player-level data remains disabled.
- Production would require official data access, legal review, and agreement.

Do not say:

- Partnership exists.
- nuLiga or OEHB approved it.
- It is live or production-ready.
- It replaces official systems today.
- Scraping or login access will be used.

## Follow-up Timing

- Send first email with pitch link, demo link, and three screenshots.
- Wait 5-7 working days.
- Send one short follow-up.
- If no response, call OEHB office or nu Datenautomaten imprint phone and ask
  for routing to the correct product/contact person.
