# Privacy and Legal Review Record

## Decision

Status: `partially-approved`

Recorded: 2026-06-01
Updated: 2026-06-05

Player-level publication is approved for reviewed adult public match reports in
this prototype when the source is an allowlisted public OEHB or nuLiga match
report page and the stored data is limited to display name, shirt number, team,
goals, seven-metre summary, card counts, and two-minute penalty count.

An anonymized public match flow may show clock, action type, team context, and
score when sourced from an allowlisted report page. It must not include player
names, player portrait links, source person IDs, officials, or referee sections.

U18 player-level publication remains disabled. Contacts, phone numbers, email
addresses, referee data, private data, portrait links, source person IDs, and
unrelated personal fields remain excluded for all competitions.

## Required Approval Before Change

A later reviewed decision must identify approver, legal basis, permitted fields,
competition categories, U18 handling, and effective date before any U18 or
non-reviewed player-level display can be enabled.
