# Privacy and Legal Review Record

## Decision

Status: `pending`

Recorded: 2026-06-01

Player-level publication is globally disabled. This includes adult and U18
competitions. No player lists, player personal data, contacts, phone numbers,
email addresses, referee data, private data, or unrelated personal fields may
be published or normalized.

An anonymized public match flow may show clock, action type, team context, and
score when sourced from an allowlisted report page. It must not include player
names, player portrait links, source person IDs, officials, or referee sections.

## Required Approval Before Change

A later reviewed decision must identify approver, legal basis, permitted fields,
competition categories, U18 handling, and effective date before any publication
profile can enable player-level display.
